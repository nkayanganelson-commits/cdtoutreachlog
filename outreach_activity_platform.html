Outreach Activity Report
UNMISS Conduct and Discipline Team · January – December 2025
UNITED NATIONS
MISSION IN SOUTH SUDAN
Conduct & Discipline Team
1. Executive Summary

This report documents outreach and awareness activities conducted by the UNMISS Conduct and Discipline Team (CDT). A total of 52 activities were carried out across 6 locations, reaching an estimated 8,243 individuals (54% female, 46% male). Activities covered SEA prevention, community-based complaint mechanisms, and UN standards of conduct.

2. Methodology

Activities were conducted through community meetings, training sessions, workshops, and school engagements. Data was collected via structured forms and aggregated through OutreachLog. Gender disaggregation was maintained across all submissions.

4. Challenges & Recommendations

Key challenges included language barriers in remote locations and seasonal accessibility constraints during the rainy season. It is recommended to expand local radio coverage, increase male engagement strategies, and deploy additional female outreach workers in areas with cultural access restrictions.
